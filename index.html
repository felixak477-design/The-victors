🛡️ Cyber Safety Centre

Small habits that help protect your devices, accounts, and personal information.

🔑

Strong passwords

Use long, unique passwords. Never share passwords or one-time codes with people who contact you unexpectedly.

🎣

Spot phishing

Pause before opening links or attachments. Check the sender and website address; urgent threats and prizes can be scams.

🔒

Protect privacy

Share only what is necessary. Ask permission before posting another person's photo, phone number, or private messages.
